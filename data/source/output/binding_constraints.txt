## Relaxing the wage condition

If a displaced worker is permitted to land at 90% of prior pay rather than
100%, how much does absorption improve?

  wage floor 100%: 17 of 33 occupations covered, 28 pathways, +6,350 gross destination capacity
  wage floor 90%: 18 of 33 occupations covered, 35 pathways, +7,770 gross destination capacity
  wage floor 80%: 19 of 33 occupations covered, 39 pathways, +10,320 gross destination capacity

Capacity is counted once per pathway here, so destinations serving several
sources are double counted. It is an upper bound.
