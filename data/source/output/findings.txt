## The contrast the report has to hold

exposed, under $60k        :  18 occs    72,380 ->   64,950    -7,430  (-10.3%)
exposed, $60k and above    :  48 occs    77,410 ->   78,300      +890  (+1.1%)

Exposure alone does not predict decline. Wage tier separates the two groups:
the same exposure scores accompany contraction below the line and growth above
it, which is why Finding 2 is posed as a question rather than a forecast.
