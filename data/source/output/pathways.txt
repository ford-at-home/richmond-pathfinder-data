occupations within the exposed set. The loss-weighted figure is what a

  of which at or above the reference wage   : +12,840
Coverage at equal-or-better pay             : 91%

Growth in screened destinations is not reserved for displaced workers; it is
hiring the whole regional labor market competes for. Treat the coverage figure
as an upper bound on what unassisted absorption could achieve.
