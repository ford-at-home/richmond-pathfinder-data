  The OEWS claim is a -8,780 change in office and administrative support
  employment, roughly -1.3% of total metro employment, inside a total that
  grew. QCEW has no occupational dimension, so it cannot confirm or refute
  that directly; it can only show whether the industries that employ the
  most clerical workers moved in a consistent direction.
