# Threshold sensitivity — Richmond MSA exposure analysis

Two cut points appear in the report: observed exposure at or above 0.25,
and a location quotient at or above 1.10. Neither is designated as
meaningful by the source data or by prior research. This recomputes each
headline result across a range of alternatives.

## 4. The wage separation

Employment-weighted mean wage of exposed occupations against the least
exposed (0.05 or below), at each cut point. This is the finding reviewers
identified as strongest, so its robustness matters most.

The premium is positive at every threshold tested, so the wage separation
does not depend on the choice of 0.25.
