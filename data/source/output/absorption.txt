# Absorption capacity — Richmond MSA, 2023 to 2025

Displaced population: occupations with observed exposure at or above 0.25
that lost employment between May 2023 and May 2025.

Destination: an occupation with exposure below 0.15, at least 500 jobs,
employment growing over the same period, and a mean wage at or above the
reference wage. Adjacent destinations are the subset that O*NET records as a
primary related occupation for at least one displaced occupation, in either
direction.

## Sensitivity to the adjacency assumption

Reading O*NET's relatedness as published, without adding reverse edges,
narrows the reachable set further. The finding does not depend on the choice.

Population                                   Symmetric  As published
All declining exposed occupations               +4,830        +3,170
Office and administrative support               +1,270          +330

The region generated nearly enough durable, better-paid hiring to cover the
jobs it lost. Almost none of that hiring is adjacent to the people who lost
them. The constraint is the distance between the two, not the volume of
either. Capacity is also not reserved for displaced workers, so these are
upper bounds on what could be absorbed without intervention.
