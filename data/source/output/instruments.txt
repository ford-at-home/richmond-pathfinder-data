## 5. The self-employment blind spot

OEWS is an establishment survey of wage and salary workers in nonfarm
establishments. It excludes the self-employed, owner-operators and unpaid
family workers. Every destination in this report is therefore a payroll job.
Licensed trades are the destination class most often entered through
owner-operation, so the capacity measured above understates the trades
specifically, and by an amount this data cannot bound.
